Wikimania, the annual conference of the Wikipedia and other Wikimedia projects, was held in Montreal, Canada during August 11–13, 2017. Sailesh Patnaik and Subhashish Panigrahi of O Foundation were awarded Wikimania scholarships this year for their contribution to Wikimedia projects, participated in this event, and divided their time discussing about First Nations language Wikimedia projects in a panel at BAnQ Vieux-Montréal, conducting a workshop on audio recording tool for Wiktionary and two lightning talks, and setting up a booth at the community village in collaboration with [Odia Wikimedians User Group](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Odia_Wikimedians_User_Group), and [Wikitongues](http://wikitongues.org/). and contributed towards several workshops and talks. The Odia Wikimedians User Group, Wikitongues, and we together set up a booth in the Wikimania Community Village. Our participation at Wikimania 2017:

- [Edit-o-thon: First Nations on Wikipedia](https://ca.wikimedia.org/wiki/Edit-o-thon:_First_Nations_on_Wikipedia#Agenda) (Subhashish participated as part of a panel at [Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec](http://%5BSearch%20domain%20www.banq.qc.ca%5D%20https://www.banq.qc.ca) (BAnQ Vieux-Montréal)
- [WikiTungi’s efforts for localising global projects](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Learning_and_Evaluation/News/Learning_Days_for_Wikimania_Montreal/Thursday#Lightning_Talks) (Lightning talk by Sailesh)
- [Kathabhidhana: Recording words for Wiktionary and preparing for an AI assistant](https://wikimania2017.wikimedia.org/wiki/Submissions/Kathabhidhana:_Recording_words_for_Wiktionary_and_preparing_for_an_AI_assistant "Submissions/Kathabhidhana: Recording words for Wiktionary and preparing for an AI assistant") (workshop by Subhashish)
- [8 FLOSS tools and 4 hardware for audio, video and image editing for Commons](https://openspeaks.com/floss-tools-image-audio-video-and-hardware-for-wikimedia-commons/ "Submissions/8 FLOSS tools and 4 hardware for audio, video and image editing for Commons") (lightning talk by Subhashish. See [submission](https://wikimania2017.wikimedia.org/wiki/Submissions/8_FLOSS_tools_and_4_hardware_for_audio,_video_and_image_editing_for_Commons))

*We thank Wikimedia Foundation for offering the Wikimania Scholarship to Sailesh and Subhashish that enabled us to participate in this great event.*
