The Ho language is native to over 1.4 million people who live mostly in the Indian states of Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha, and West Bengal, and Bangladesh. Though Ho has historically been written in Devanagari, Bengali, Odia, and Latin alphabet, Warang Citi is the most accepted script that was created by Lako Bodra, a community leader from Jharkhand. The language has been identified as a vulnerable language by [UNESCO](http://www.unesco.org/languages-atlas/).

Ho written in Warang Citi alphabet:

There is currently an active [Wikipedia Incubator project](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/hoc/%F0%91%A2%B6%F0%91%A3%81%F0%91%A3%8A_%F0%91%A2%BE%F0%91%A3%8C%F0%91%A3%88%F0%91%A3%93) where Ho speakers can create and edit Wikipedia articles. The Ho Wikipedia is yet to go live but with more contributors joining [**here**](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/hoc/%F0%91%A2%B6%F0%91%A3%81%F0%91%A3%8A_%F0%91%A2%BE%F0%91%A3%8C%F0%91%A3%88%F0%91%A3%93) and contributing encyclopedic content will make this a reality. In general, more content on the Internet is extremely important to share knowledge with the native speakers.\

#### **Resources:**

- [Ho-language textbooks and dictionary](http://ho.bharatavani.in/text-book/) (subjects include Mathematics, Biology Science, Kagol, Poetry, Political Science, Sangam Sagam, Science Kaira, Snagam and World History; Text from two publishers — Ho Language Education Council, Odisha and Institute of Ancient Culture and Science Society, Odisha). Bharatvani, Ministry of Human Resources Development, Government of India
- [**Ho Wikipedia Test Incubator**](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/hoc/%F0%91%A2%B6%F0%91%A3%81%F0%91%A3%8A_%F0%91%A2%BE%F0%91%A3%8C%F0%91%A3%88%F0%91%A3%93)
- [Ho-language talking dictionary, video of a teacher teaching the Warang Citi alphabet, and other resources](http://www.swarthmore.edu/SocSci/langhotspots/Ho/index.html) by Swarthmore College Laboratory for Endangered Languages Research and Documentation
- [Warang Citi Unicode chart](https://www.unicode.org/charts/PDF/U118A0.pdf) (particularly useful for typeface designers)
- [Aksharamukha converter](https://aksharamukha.appspot.com/#/converter) (helps convert Warang Citi↔other writing systems)
- Fonts:
  - [Saning Disum Sans Regular](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B5__qJoEj3L8bDFaU2RTakxvMVE/view)
  - [BoyoGagraiUnicode Regular](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B5__qJoEj3L8Mk50bEJEX0ZHRm8/view)
- [Source code](https://github.com/wikimedia/jquery.ime/tree/master/rules/hoc) for Input tool to type in Ho (currently used on Wikipedia and other Wikimedia projects)
- [Education in Ho](http://holanguageodishaedu.in/school_college.html) (schools and colleges)
