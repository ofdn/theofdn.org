*This page first appeared on [OpenSpeaks](http://openspeaks.com/languages/ho/) in 2019. It is also in Ho, written in Warang Citi. If your device does not show Warang Citi, install one of the fonts under “Read and type”.*

The Ho language is spoken by over 1.4 million people, mostly in the Indian states of Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha and West Bengal, and in Bangladesh. Ho has been written in Devanagari, Bengali, Odia and Latin scripts. Warang Citi (𑢹𑣗𑣁𑣜𑣊 𑣏𑣂𑣕𑣂), created by Lako Bodra, a community leader from Jharkhand, is now the most widely accepted script. UNESCO lists Ho as a vulnerable language.

![Icon for Wikipedia links to pages in the Ho language (hoc).](/assets/logos/iso-639-ho.webp)

Ho speakers are writing a Ho Wikipedia on the [Wikimedia Incubator](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/hoc). It will go live once more people write articles there. More writing in Ho on the internet helps Ho speakers find and share knowledge in their own language.

Mangu Purty made a series of video tutorials on contributing to the Ho Wikipedia Incubator. They are under “Watch and listen”, and you can also [download them from Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Instructional_videos_on_using_Wikipedia_in_Ho).
