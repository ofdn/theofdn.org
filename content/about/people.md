### [Jnanaranjan Sahu](https://jsahu.me/)

Technology

Jnanaranjan leads all things technology at OFDN. A technology consultant by profession and a Wikipedian as a volunteer, he has led the development of numerous Open Source tools—including input tools and encoding converters in Ho, Odia and Santali languages.

### Dr. Nasim Ali

Technology

Utilizing technology for the indigenisation of web and other electronic media. Involved in developing tools that empower local linguistic communities to fully harness the power of digital tech. Contributions include ongoing work on developing several families of high quality Odia fonts, typing tools for Odia and Ol-Chiki and an OCR for Odia.

### Prateek Pattanaik

research & media

Prateek is an interdisciplinary researcher with a focus on digitally-documenting underrepresented tangible and intangible cultural artefacts; led documenting many rare performing arts like *Prahallada Nataka* and Bharata Lila. Prateek leads the “[Pothi](/activities/pothi/)” project at OFDN which covers performing arts, rare books & manuscripts, heritage monuments and musical traditions through archives, research, translations and educational resources. ### Ramjit Tudu

community Tech

Ramjit helped shape the Santali Wikipedia which also happens to be the first South Asian indigenous-language Wikipedia. He manages the [Marginalized Community Council](/activities/marginalized-community-council/)—a working group that works in the intersections of marginalised communities and issues related to their languages and cultures, technology, geopolitics and education—at OFDN.

[Twitter ](https://twitter.com/Ramjit_Tudu) [Github ](https://github.com/hpnrt) ![Sailesh Patnaik](/assets/images/people-sailesh-patnaik.webp)

#### Partners

![Whose Knowledge? logo](/assets/logos/whose-knowledge.webp)

### Whose Knowledge?

**[Whose Knowledge?](https://whoseknowledge.org/)** is a global multi-lingual campaign that is working to correct the skewed representations of knowledge on the Internet. Founded by Anasuya Sengupta and Siko Bouterse, Whose Knowledge? has been instrumental in identifying several “knowledge gaps” that exist on the Internet.

[Twitter ](https://twitter.com/pattaprateek) [Globe ](https://pattaprateek.com/) [Youtube ](https://youtube.com/pattaprateek) [Github ](https://github.com/pattaprateek) ![Wikitongues_logo](/assets/logos/wikitongues.webp)

### Wikitongues

**[Wikitongues](https://wikitongues.org)** is a non-profit platform for every language in the world. In coordination with an international community of contributors, we collect [oral histories](https://youtube.com/wikitongues) from the world’s more than 7,000 communities. We also develop tools and tactics for activists to preserve their languages, such as [Poly](https://poly.wikitongues.org), open source software for building word lists.

Facebook Twitter Dribbble Behance ![OpenwordsLogo300px. Copyrighted by OpenWords](/assets/logos/openwords.webp)

### Openwords

**[OpenWords](http://openwords.com/)** is an educational and social enterprise based in the US that builds high quality lessons for any language, and builds Open Source tools for educators and universities. OpenWords WebApp, its flagship project is an Open Source language learning app that has already made its way to Indiana University. OpenWords’ founder Marc Bogonovich is a dear friend and advisor to us.

[Twitter ](https://twitter.com/openwords) [Globe ](https://openwords.com/) [![Council of Analytical Tribal Studies (COATS) logo](/assets/logos/coats.webp)](http://www.coatskoraput.co.in/)

### [Council of Analytical Tribal Studies](http://www.coatskoraput.co.in/)

[Council of Analytical Tribal Studies](https://coatskoraput.org/) (COATS) is a research and academic institute in Koraput, Odisha, India that was founded in 1993 to dive deeper into the life of indigenous communities in the region. Since then COATS has done ground-breaking work by bringing many researcher scholars and academics under one roof both for full time research with their M.A., M.Phil and PhD programs, many year-round activities, and publications.

[Link ](http://www.coatskoraput.co.in/)

#### Advisors

### Anasuya Sengupta

WhoseKnowledge?

Anasuya Sengupta is the co-founder, and currently Co-Director of Whose Knowledge?. With over two decades of leadership in development sectors across global South, and worldwide, Anasuya has made significant contribution in amplifying marginalised voices in both digital and real worlds. A former Chief Grantmaking Officer at the Wikimedia Foundation and a 2017 Shuttleworth Fellow, Anasuya has been as an advisor to us since the beginning of OFDN.
