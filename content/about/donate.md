Where does your donation money go?

We, at O Foundation (OFDN) do not make any profit. We help build open resources and communities to transform our marginalised languages and cultures to a digital space.

Everything costs money — from maintaining infrastructures, to visiting communities, to recording, and to spending hours in coding and for post-production of recorded media. That’s just a glimpse of things that we do. One of the ways we can sustain our work is by asking you to donate how much ever you can.

You can choose to be anonymous or have your name listed as a donor when you donate. We will try to attribute for your kind support appropriately.

Thanks!\
[Core team](/people/), OFDN
