All tech is human, but all humans don’t have equal access.

## Community media

We study community media, co-create openly licensed media content, and document our approach and process.

## Open tech

We hack into and build open and community-led tech, and share how we fail, learn and succeed.

## Media, tech and power

We analyse media and tech through prior power structures of race, caste, gender and disabilities.

## New films

Our recent documentary films *The Volunteer Archivists*, *Nani Ma* and *Gyani Maiya* capture resilience of communities and collectives. [More films](/film/)
