Our society in general has been historically patriarchal. The internet is skewed the way the information is created and shared. It is also quite English-centric. Bringing a balance of content and participation is one of our major focus areas. We are conducting several researches to map and identify the marginalised areas on the Internet so that we can streamline our efforts to bring that balance.
