These policies apply to every page on this site. Some pages have their own licence or attribution. If they do, that page says so.

## Privacy

This site has no comments, accounts, analytics or advertising. We do not set cookies.

- **Theme.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice. It stays on your device.
- **Hosting.** GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log your IP address for security. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Fonts.** The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google a request for them. See the [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
- **Embedded media.** Some pages embed players from Vimeo, the Internet Archive, Spotify and SoundCloud, or media from Wikimedia Commons. These services may collect data about you as if you visited their sites. YouTube videos load only when you press play.
- **Contact form.** Our [contact form](/reach-out/) is a Google Form. We keep what you send only to reply to you. Ask us, and we will delete it.

## Safe space policy and code of conduct

This policy applies to everyone who takes part in our work, online and offline. That includes our social media channels.

- **Mutual respect and equity.** We want everyone who works with us to feel safe and welcome. Treat each other with respect. We take harassment and bullying seriously. By following this policy, you agree that we may ask the police for help when a situation is beyond our control.
- **Individual and community privacy.** Respect the privacy of other people and groups. Do not photograph or film anyone, or publish personal information about any person or group, without their consent.
- **Respect for the environment and communities.** Respect nature. Respect the physical and digital spaces of the communities we work with.
