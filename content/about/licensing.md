[**CODE**](http://github.com/ofdn)

We strive to release the source code of the software that we produce under a [GNU GFDL](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl.html) license but at times we release with other suitable open licenses too. For instance, it makes more sense to release a font under a [SIL Open Font License (OFL)](http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&id=OFL). See our [git](http://github.com/ofdn) to check our entire repository. Our other project—[OpenSpeaks](https://openspeaks.com/)—has its code hosted and maintained in a [separate repo](https://github.com/OpenSpeaks).

**DATA**

Our data is always released under a [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/choose/zero/) license (comparable to Public Domain).

**ATTRIBUTION\**
Our site is [made with WordPress](http://wordpress.org/) with the theme [Magnus Maximus.](http://thefivethemes.com) Generally images that are used in different pages and posts are attributed in the respective pages. Anything that is not attributed can be assumed to be created by us and published under a CC-BY 4.0 license (as explained in the “content” section above).
