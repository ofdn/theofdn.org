*This page was first published in August 2018, when the Santali Wikipedia went live.*

Santali (ᱥᱟᱱᱛᱟᱲᱤ) is spoken mostly in the Indian states of Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Mizoram, Odisha, Tripura and West Bengal, and in Bangladesh, Bhutan and Nepal. The 2011 Census of India counted 7,368,192 speakers. Ol Chiki (ᱚᱞ ᱪᱤᱠᱤ), also called Ol Cemet', is the main script for Santali. Raghunath Murmu created it in 1925. Santali has also been written in Devanagari, Bengali and Odia.

Santali is one of the few Adivasi languages in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution. Schools in Jharkhand, Odisha and West Bengal teach it. This page lists free resources for reading, typing and writing Santali in Ol Chiki. If Ol Chiki shows as empty boxes on your screen, install a font from [Read and type](#resources-type) and reload the page.

Members of O Foundation helped build Santali input tools and typefaces, and helped grow the community of Santali Wikipedia editors.
